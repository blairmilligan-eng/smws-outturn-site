SMWS Code 24: Macallan

Distillery behind Society cask numbers 24.x

SMWS code 24 is Macallan, in the Speyside region. It is an operating distillery, founded in 1824. Any Society bottling numbered 24.x (for example 24.1, 24.42) is a single cask from Macallan.

About Macallan

Above the Spey at Easter Elchies, in a manor house that predates it by a century, Macallan has made whisky since 1824 and turned sherried Speyside into both an art and an obsession. Raised in the finest Spanish oloroso oak, its spirit runs deep with dried fruit, spice and old polished wood, and its rarest bottles change hands for the price of cars. In 2018 it eased a spectacular wave-roofed distillery into the hillside, as though the ground itself had risen. Edrington's crown jewel.

The Scotch Malt Whisky Society bottles single casks under numeric codes rather than distillery names, so a label reading 24.403 means cask 403 from distillery 24. That distillery is Macallan, in Speyside. More on Macallan at Wikipedia.
