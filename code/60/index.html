SMWS Code 60: Aberfeldy

Distillery behind Society cask numbers 60.x

SMWS code 60 is Aberfeldy, in the Highland region. It is an operating distillery, founded in 1896. Any Society bottling numbered 60.x (for example 60.1, 60.42) is a single cask from Aberfeldy.

About Aberfeldy

Beside the tumbling Pitilie Burn in highland Perthshire, John Dewar & Sons raised Aberfeldy in 1896 as the beating heart of their blends. The whisky carries a famous streak of heather-honey sweetness, golden and warming, and the distillery remains one of the friendliest doors in Scotch to walk through. Still Dewar's (Bacardi).

Notes from an SMWS member

I've visited Aberfeldy and had the pleasure of meeting Stephanie MacLeod, the multi-award-winning master blender whose work shapes its honeyed, heather-sweet Highland style. Sitting beside the Pitilie Burn in Perthshire, it's one of the warmest, most welcoming distilleries I've been to — and tasting the spirit where it's made gives the SMWS bottlings a whole new context.

First-hand notes from Karyn and Blair.

The Scotch Malt Whisky Society bottles single casks under numeric codes rather than distillery names, so a label reading 60.403 means cask 403 from distillery 60. That distillery is Aberfeldy, in Highland. More on Aberfeldy at Wikipedia.
