SMWS Code 10: Bunnahabhain

Distillery behind Society cask numbers 10.x

SMWS code 10 is Bunnahabhain, in the Islay region. It is an operating distillery, founded in 1881. Any Society bottling numbered 10.x (for example 10.1, 10.42) is a single cask from Bunnahabhain.

About Bunnahabhain

“Bunnahabhain distillery was founded in 1881 near Port Askaig on Islay and is owned by the Scotch whisky producer Distell Group Limited a subsidiary of Heineken N.V,. The village of Bunnahabhain was founded to house its workers.”From Wikipedia, CC BY-SA

The Scotch Malt Whisky Society bottles single casks under numeric codes rather than distillery names, so a label reading 10.403 means cask 403 from distillery 10. That distillery is Bunnahabhain, in Islay. More on Bunnahabhain at Wikipedia.
