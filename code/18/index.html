SMWS Code 18: Inchgower

Distillery behind Society cask numbers 18.x

SMWS code 18 is Inchgower, in the Speyside region. It is an operating distillery, founded in 1871. Any Society bottling numbered 18.x (for example 18.1, 18.42) is a single cask from Inchgower.

About Inchgower

On the Moray coast at the edge of Buckie, where Speyside meets the sea, Inchgower has stood since 1871. This is a coastal Speysider, and it tastes like one, a firm, slightly salty, waxy spirit with a nutty edge that sets it apart from the softer valley malts inland. Owned by Diageo, it has spent most of its life as a backbone of blends, above all Bell's, and bottlings under its own name are comparatively rare, which is part of the quiet thrill of meeting it as a single cask. A working distillery, unshowy, shaped by the salt air off the Moray Firth.

The Scotch Malt Whisky Society bottles single casks under numeric codes rather than distillery names, so a label reading 18.403 means cask 403 from distillery 18. That distillery is Inchgower, in Speyside. More on Inchgower at Wikipedia.
