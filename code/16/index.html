SMWS Code 16: Glenturret

Distillery behind Society cask numbers 16.x

SMWS code 16 is Glenturret, in the Highland region. It is an operating distillery. Any Society bottling numbered 16.x (for example 16.1, 16.42) is a single cask from Glenturret.

About Glenturret

At Crieff in Perthshire, Glenturret lays claim to being one of the oldest working distilleries in Scotland, with roots reaching deep into the 18th century. It has kept faith with the old, handmade ways, mashing by hand and working at a human scale that has all but vanished elsewhere. For years it was the home of the Famous Grouse blend's visitor experience; today, under the Lalique Group, it has stepped forward as a single malt in its own right, dressed in crystal and ambition. The whisky is rich, waxy and traditional, the taste of a distillery that never stopped doing things the slow way.

The Scotch Malt Whisky Society bottles single casks under numeric codes rather than distillery names, so a label reading 16.403 means cask 403 from distillery 16. That distillery is Glenturret, in Highland. More on Glenturret at Wikipedia.
