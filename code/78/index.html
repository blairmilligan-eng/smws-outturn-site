SMWS Code 78: Ben Nevis

Distillery behind Society cask numbers 78.x

SMWS code 78 is Ben Nevis, in the Highland region. It is an operating distillery, founded in 1825. Any Society bottling numbered 78.x (for example 78.1, 78.42) is a single cask from Ben Nevis.

About Ben Nevis

Under the shoulder of Britain's highest mountain at Fort William, Long John MacDonald founded Ben Nevis in 1825. Its spirit is big, characterful and, at times, wonderfully wild, a distillery with a cult all its own. Owned since 1989 by Japan's Nikka, a quiet marriage of Highland and Japanese sensibilities.

The Scotch Malt Whisky Society bottles single casks under numeric codes rather than distillery names, so a label reading 78.403 means cask 403 from distillery 78. That distillery is Ben Nevis, in Highland. More on Ben Nevis at Wikipedia.
