About Loch Lomond (Rhosdhu)

A curiosity from Loch Lomond at Alexandria: spirit made entirely from malted barley, but distilled in a Coffey column still rather than a pot. Because Scotch rules reserve the words single malt for pot stills, it must be called single grain. The result has a malt's depth with a column still's polish, floral and fruity. Loch Lomond Group's.
