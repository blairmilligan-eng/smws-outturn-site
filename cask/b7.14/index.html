About MGP (Midwest Grain Products)

At Lawrenceburg, Indiana, on the Ohio River, the old Ross & Squibb distillery dates from 1847 and was Seagram's for most of the twentieth century. MGP bought it in 2011, and its whiskey now fills bottles under some fifty different brand names, often without saying where it came from. This is its bourbon, clean, sweet and well made. MGP's.
