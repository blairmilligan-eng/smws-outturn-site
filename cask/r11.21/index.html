About Worthy Park

In the heart of Jamaica, Worthy Park was granted in 1670, made sugar from 1720 and rum for some two centuries until it stopped in the early 1960s. In 2005 Gordon Clarke, of the family that has owned it since 1918, built a modern distillery on the estate, and the rum returned in 2007. Single estate, heavy and fruity. Clarke family's.
